STEP 01

Choose references for what you like.

Add photos with makeup in your selected category you would like to wear. Select the makeup region in each photo, then review the extracted shades together.

Up to 6 images per set. JPEG, PNG or WebP; up to 10 MB and 40 megapixels each. Crop tightly to avoid teeth, skin and reflections.

Keep checked only the shades you like. Continuing saves all checked shades as likes; unchecked shades are ignored.

STEP 02 · OPTIONAL

Would you like to add an aesthetic reference?

This can represent a friend group, a partner, a workplace or an aesthetic you want to fit. You decide the influence.

Up to 6 images. These represent your estimate of this audience’s taste, not verified feedback from them. Review each makeup region and keep the relevant shades checked.

STEP 03

Your color vision profile

Enter the type and severity stated in your diagnosis. Personal matching uses an approximate simulation with mild, moderate and severe presets of 0.33, 0.66 and 1.0.

This stores your entered diagnosis for the future CVD module; it does not yet change recommendations.
